# PMT-score-check
For every aspiring doctor, the Punjab Medical Test (PMT) is more than just an exam—it’s a milestone. Checking your score is the first step toward understanding your performance and planning the path ahead.
The <a href="https://8171ehsaasprogram.net/176/pmt-score/">PMT score check</a> not only shows marks but also highlights areas where you can improve, giving you a clearer direction for your medical or dental college admission goals.
